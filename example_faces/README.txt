EXAMPLE FACES FOR TESTING
=========================
These people DO NOT EXIST. Each base face was generated by AI (StyleGAN,
thispersondoesnotexist.com). The 15 photos per person were made from it with
FaceFusion's face editor (head turns, expressions) + face enhancer.

person_A, person_B, person_C   15 photos each, all pass the Studio photo check.
_base                         the original generated faces. base1, base4, base5
                              (cap, sunglasses, cap+glasses) are examples of
                              photos you should NOT use.

Use: Studio step 3 -> "Add photos" -> select all 15 photos of ONE person.
     (Remove your own photos first, or they mix into one blended face.)
Make a new set: tools/env/bin/python example_faces/make_examples.py <face.jpg> person_D
